Ἀκροωμένοις was created with the intent of doing what Legentibus does (and more) but for Greek texts: text/audio allignment, dictionary lookups, commentary, parallel translation, UI customization, etc. We intend to make the reading of ancient Greek as easy and enjoyable as possible.
